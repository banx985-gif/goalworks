GOALWORKS Privacy Policy

Banx Games · Effective 14 November 2026

The short version

  • Your clubs are saved on your own phone or computer. We never receive them.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We don't collect your name, email or any other personal data.
  • The Android app can show ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your phone's advertising ID to pick them, and you can choose general ads instead.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Reminders (if you turn them on) are made on your own phone. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Who we are

GOALWORKS is a football club management game made by Banx Games, a small independent developer. This policy covers the GOALWORKS app for Android and the GOALWORKS web version. Every club, player and competition in the game is fictional. If you have any question about this policy, email banx985@gmail.com.

What the game stores

GOALWORKS saves your clubs (four campaign slots), your account record (achievements, the Hall of Fame, secrets found, Club Tokens), your settings and your answer about ads in the storage of the device you play on. If the game ever hits an error, a short note about it (what went wrong, not who you are) is kept in that same save so you can copy it for us if you want to; it is never sent anywhere by itself. This data stays on that device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no server that could receive it.

You can remove it at any time: delete a slot in the game, uninstall the app, or clear the app's (or the web page's) stored data. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your saves are gone for good, because no copy exists anywhere else.

No account, no personal data from us

GOALWORKS has no account, no sign-in and no chat. We (Banx Games) don't collect, receive or sell any personal data, and the game contains no analytics or tracking of our own.

Ads (Android app)

The Android app is free and may show ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a small in-game reward, and an occasional ad between screens. Ads never change a match, a player or a secret. The web version shows no real ads. Until GOALWORKS is released on Google Play, the app shows Google's test ads only.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's AdMob software in the app may collect information from your device, such as your phone's advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address and approximate location worked out from it, which ads were shown and whether you tapped them, and basic device and app information. This information goes to Google, not to us. On first launch the app asks how ads may be picked: ads that may be personalised using your advertising ID, or general (non-personalised) ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland this question is Google's own consent form instead, and no ad is requested before you answer it. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy & legal → Ad choices.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (Club Token packs and Remove Ads: fixed packs and a one-time purchase, no subscriptions, no random packs) are sold and handled by Google Play. You pay Google, under Google Play's terms, and your payment details are never shared with us. The app only receives Google Play's confirmation of what you bought, and keeps that on your device. Refunds are managed in the Google Play app. Nothing for sale changes a match result, a player's ability or a secret: the whole game can be finished without paying.

Notifications

GOALWORKS never asks to send notifications when you first open it. After your first win, Sam (your assistant manager) asks once in the game whether you would like a few reminders, such as a match coming up or a reward waiting. Only if you say yes does the app ask your phone (or browser) for permission. The reminders are local notifications: the game makes and schedules them on your own device (at most 3 a day, never between 10 pm and 8 am). There is no notification server and nothing about you is sent anywhere. You can switch them off, all together or one kind at a time, in Settings → Notifications.

The web version

The web version is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard server records (such as your IP address and browser type) when the game's files are loaded. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

GOALWORKS is suitable for all ages and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will help.

Phone features the app uses

The app uses your phone's vibration for feedback (Settings can turn it off) and local notifications if you allow them. It uses the internet only to load ads and to talk to Google Play for purchases. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Terms of use

GOALWORKS is provided as it is, for your personal entertainment. Club Tokens and other in-game items have no money value and can't be exchanged for money. Please don't try to cheat the store or copy and resell the game or its art. Purchases are covered by Google Play's terms and refund rules. The game, its art, characters and music are © Banx Games. Every club, player and competition in it is fictional; any likeness to a real one is a coincidence.

Open-source parts: the Android app is built with Capacitor (MIT licence) and its plugins (MIT licence), and Google's AdMob and Play Billing libraries under Google's terms. The game's own code uses no other outside libraries.
